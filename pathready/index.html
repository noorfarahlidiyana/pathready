          Frequently asked questions

          Quick answers about how PathReady works and how it treats your data.

          Who is PathReady for?
          UTP students and recent graduates in Information Systems, Information Technology, Computer Science, Computer Engineering and Business Management who want to know how ready they are for roles such as data, software, cloud, security, business analysis and more.
          Do I need to create an account?
          No. You can start straight away. Your saved assessments and action plan are stored in your own browser on this device.
          What happens to my resume?
          Your resume text is used to fill in the form and write the screening report. It is not saved. If the server is set up with an AI provider key, the text is sent to that provider for analysis; otherwise a simpler keyword method on the server is used. Do not upload a resume you are not allowed to share.
          Which file types can I upload?
          PDF or plain text, up to 5 MB. Scanned PDFs contain no readable text, so paste the text into the box instead.
          How accurate is my readiness score?
          Treat it as guidance, not a prediction of any job offer.
          • The score comes from transparent demonstration rules, or from a baseline model trained on synthetic data when the API is running. It has not been validated against real hiring outcomes.
          • Skills are self-reported, and AI-filled values are estimates, so review them before analysing.
          Is the what-if simulation a guarantee?
          No. It estimates how your score may change if you improve, based on the current model. It does not guarantee an internship or a job.
